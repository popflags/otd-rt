## What's new in 2.12.0

- **Touchdown Gate** (experimental, new setting, default 500 ms): a touchdown (the pen coming down from the air) clicks no sooner
  than this long after the previous touchdown's click. A touchdown that lands earlier clicks exactly then.
  - It applies to taps and drags alike; at touchdown the plugin cannot tell which one it will be.
  - It counts from the previous click, so tapping faster than the gate falls behind: each held click pushes the next one.
  - Re-presses without lifting and releases are not delayed.
  - A tap that already ended when its click goes out is clicked for as long as it lasted.
  - A touch that lands while a click is waiting joins that click.
  - It runs on the PC clock. A timer sends the click if no report arrives in time (pen out of range).
  - 0 turns it off; the plugin then behaves exactly like 2.11.1.
- Diagnostics CSVs gain a `detected` column (the detector's event). `pressed` / `event` are what the plugin sent.

## Settings to change (coming from 2.11.1)

- **Touchdown Gate**: 500 to try it (the default), 0 to keep 2.11.1 behaviour. On the 2026-10-20 log, drags that follow a tap landed
  a median 432 ms after it, so with 500 most of them start ~100 ms later. Lower the value (480, 450) if that matters.

Replace `RapidTrigger.dll` (Windows: `%localappdata%\OpenTabletDriver\Plugins\<folder>\`) and restart OpenTabletDriver.
