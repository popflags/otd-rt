## What's new in 2.11.1

- A real tap right after a firmware phantom (a lone 8191 reading just after a lift) now presses on its first sample. Before, the
  dropout guard held the zero between them and the tap pressed one sample (2 ms) late. Found in a live diagnostics session: the only
  press in 935 that came later than its first sample apart from a tap that landed at exactly 8191.

## Settings to change (coming from 2.11.0)

None. Replace `RapidTrigger.dll` (Windows: `%localappdata%\OpenTabletDriver\Plugins\<folder>\`) and restart OpenTabletDriver.
